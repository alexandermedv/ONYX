# Collection Availability Decision — Avito v2.3

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Decision:** Business and Lifestyle are `PUBLIC_LAUNCH_AVAILABLE` for the ONYX soft launch.

## Evidence

- P02 Lifestyle contains ten distinct 3:4 final candidates covering café, city, home, reading, rooftop, weekend, balcony, sofa, travel and evening scenes.
- All ten PNG files decode fully, have unique SHA256 values and have byte-identical source/final staging copies.
- The package includes prompts, source mapping, technical QA, web derivatives and marketing layouts. Visual review of the contact sheet found no collection-level defect that prevents ONYX from offering Lifestyle as a production direction.
- Human scoring of the exact images remains `PENDING`; this does not grant publication rights to those files.

## Public scope

- **Business** — деловые портреты для резюме, профессионального профиля, сайта и личного бренда.
- **Lifestyle** — естественные современные фотографии для соцсетей, личного профиля и повседневного образа.

Portrait, Signature and Premium may use either available Collection after the source photographs and creative task are checked. Prices remain 1 000 ₽, 3 000 ₽ and 5 000 ₽. Premium remains one main Collection; a complete Business session plus a complete Lifestyle session is separate scope.

Executive, Fashion, Glamour, Travel, Evening, Dating, Boudoir and all other directions remain `NOT_PUBLICLY_LAUNCHED` until a separate recorded decision.

## Asset-rights boundary

P02 Lifestyle is a synthetic persona with traceable identity references and collection manifests. The exact Lifestyle finals and derived marketing files have `marketing_approved: false`, `avito_publication_approved: false` and `publish_approved: false`. Owner approval is the remaining rights gate for using any of those exact visuals publicly.

Avito v2.3 therefore states that Lifestyle is available as a service but uses no Lifestyle image. Card 03 is an editorial, typographic comparison of Business and Lifestyle.
