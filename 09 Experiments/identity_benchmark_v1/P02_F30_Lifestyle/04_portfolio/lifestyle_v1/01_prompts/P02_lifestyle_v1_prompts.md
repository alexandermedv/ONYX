# P02 LIFESTYLE V1 — prompts

## Session identity rule

Use MASTER and REF01–REF05 strictly for identity, facial structure, natural body proportions and appearance.

Do NOT preserve or mechanically copy the pose, head angle, gaze direction, facial expression, hand position, hairstyle arrangement, clothing or composition from the references.

Each image must feel like a genuinely different moment from the same person's life.

Intentionally vary:
- facial expression
- gaze direction
- head angle
- body orientation
- camera distance
- hairstyle arrangement
- activity
- hand position
- lighting
- environment

Avoid repeatedly using the same three-quarter face angle and subtle closed-mouth smile.
