# ADR-0009 — Commercial Product Freeze v1

Date: 2026-09-20. Status: ACCEPTED for commercial documentation/config; operational blockers remain explicit.

## Context

Production sources disagreed on entry product, Preview credits, payment timing, Premium deliverables and taxonomy. Existing user work and historical assets must remain intact. R&D is not authority to revise commercial promises.

## Decision

Keep [ONYX_PRODUCT_SYSTEM.md](../13%20Production/Product_Standards/ONYX_PRODUCT_SYSTEM.md) at its existing path as the single commercial authority, with logical ID ONYX_COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT_SYSTEM_v1. Price Book and products_v1.yaml mirror it. Freeze 2026-09-20: Portrait/Signature/Premium 1000/3000/5000 RUB for 1/10/20 accepted finals; one main Collection with Concept/Scene hierarchy. Add-ons and upgrades follow the source; Review v1.1 after 10 unique paid orders.

Preserve Signature/Premium config mappings; retain Preview only as explicitly SUPERSEDED, legacy-only and NOT_FOR_SALE. Existing historical orders and renderer Preview input contract remain unchanged. New intake uses PORTRAIT and stable commercial IDs; taxonomy manifest fields are guidance, not a migration of runtime contracts.

## Consequences

Motion and specialized social exports cease to be mandatory frozen Premium deliverables; legacy agreements are not retroactively altered. Additional Concept buys creative scope, extra finals remain explicit. Urgency quotes record their base/deadline, and upgrades record delivery-date window, credits and cumulative entitlements. These operational clarifications are recorded in the source rather than hidden in code.

No generation, external runtime modification, client asset change, upload or automation integration. Retention/deletion and final payment/refund operations require owner decisions; readiness is conditional. [Version history](../13%20Production/Product_Standards/ONYX_COMMERCIAL_VERSION_HISTORY_v1.md) preserves replaced texts and explicit value transitions.
