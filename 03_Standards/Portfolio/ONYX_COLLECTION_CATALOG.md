# ONYX Collection Catalog v1

**Status:** Product reference for Collection Book copy. Business and Lifestyle are `PUBLIC_LAUNCH_AVAILABLE` for the current soft launch, subject to pre-payment reference and creative feasibility checks. Every other entry remains catalog-only and does not imply a launched public offer.

## How the catalog works

A commercial tier defines deliverables; a Collection defines a broad visual world containing Concepts, each containing Scenes and accepted Final Images. [Product System](../Product/ONYX_PRODUCT_SYSTEM.md) governs the frozen taxonomy and public availability. Premium contains one main Collection, with compatible adjacent Concepts when coherent; a full additional Collection is separate scope. A Collection Book may recommend another collection only when it differs clearly from the book already delivered and its availability is verified. Otherwise use `Скоро` and no URL.

## Business

**Launch status:** `PUBLIC_LAUNCH_AVAILABLE`.

For professional presence where trust, clarity, and versatility matter. Typical uses: professional profile, company website, expert publication, presentation announcement, and social presence. Visual language: modern office, natural light, calm posture, direct but approachable portraiture.

The P02 sample belongs here.

## Executive

**Launch status:** `NOT_PUBLICLY_LAUNCHED`.

For founders, executives, and public experts whose work calls for a more formal status signal. It is distinct from Business through high-status environments and more formal editorial direction: CEO office, private library, financial district, premium lounge, executive meeting, and black interior. It should be recommended after Business only where that elevated positioning is a deliberate next need, not as a duplicate default.

## Lifestyle

**Launch status:** `PUBLIC_LAUNCH_AVAILABLE`.

Natural contemporary photographs for social media, a personal profile and an everyday image. Example environments may include home, café, city, leisure or travel, but no fixed scene list is promised as a deliverable.

## Dating

**Launch status:** `NOT_PUBLICLY_LAUNCHED`.

For warm, natural images in social settings: coffee shop, restaurant, sunset walk, city evening, park, rooftop, and weekend trip. It has a different intent and visual energy from Business.

## Other documented directions

Family centers on shared home and leisure scenes. Fantasy is for deliberately fictional cinematic worlds. Both are separate from professional collections and require a collection-specific brief.

## Collection Book rule

Never imply that a listed collection is currently available because it appears in this catalog. Business and Lifestyle are the only current public soft-launch Collections. Other directions must use `Скоро` or an individual availability statement and no purchase URL unless a separate decision enables them.
