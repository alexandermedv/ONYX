No exact `trial` label or explicit Business + Lifestyle Premium bundle was found in the inspected active Production product sources; the test-frame positioning and broad multi-Collection legacy `03 Product` model were found and superseded. Signature remains CORE/RECOMMENDED; it is no longer the only marketing entry point. Reference Guide's flexible quality-based rule is retained over a hard 4–8 limit.

Existing internal candidate budgets (Signature 12 / Premium 25) are unchanged and never become customer promises. Legacy proof filenames, renderer Preview enum and past sample/order facts are not current saleable products.

Config consumers for new sales must use `saleable_product_keys` (portrait/signature/premium); the retained `products.preview` entry is legacy-only and NOT_FOR_SALE. Existing schema ID is preserved, descriptive schema version advances to 1.3.

Operational clarifications introduced transparently: Additional Concept adds scope but no automatic finals; urgency base is quoted production subtotal and surcharges do not stack; upgrade window starts at source delivery and entitlements are cumulative. These are documented order-accounting rules, not runtime integration.
