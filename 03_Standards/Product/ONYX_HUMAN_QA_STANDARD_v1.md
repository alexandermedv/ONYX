# ONYX Final Human QA Standard v1

CURRENT — 2026-09-20. Applies to every tier and paid Repair. Commercial authority: [Product System](ONYX_PRODUCT_SYSTEM.md). Complements existing local quality gates; no generation or automatic PASS is introduced.

Check every deliverable for identity/likeness, natural face/eyes/teeth/hair, hands/fingers/body anatomy, clothing and objects, photorealism, absence of visible AI artifacts, scene/brief adherence, image readability, agreed dimensions/aspect ratio and sharpness. Use deterministic local checks and InsightFace identity evidence when available; an embedding score never replaces human acceptance.

For sessions, inspect duplicate/near-duplicate frames and collection diversity across Concept, Scene, framing, pose, expression, wardrobe and background. Confirm the ordered count of accepted finals and a coherent main Collection. Portrait has one agreed direction; no collection-diversity quota is imposed on one image.

Record reviewer, timestamp, candidate/final IDs, decision (PASS / REPAIR / REGENERATE / REJECT), reasons, identity evidence when available, and final acceptance. Missing reviewer approval or a critical defect prevents delivery. Human approval must refer to the current exact file/version, not a superseded candidate.

Keep source/intermediate/repaired/rejected/final separate. Free ONYX defects do not consume correction rounds. Re-check repaired/replaced outputs. Book and delivery QA must verify all accepted finals, no internal data leaks and clean client files; Collection Book visual approval is a separate gate. Existing order review evidence remains unchanged.
