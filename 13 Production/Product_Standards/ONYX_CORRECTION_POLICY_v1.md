# ONYX Correction Policy v1

CURRENT — 2026-09-20. Commercial authority: [Product System](ONYX_PRODUCT_SYSTEM.md).

| Classification | Examples | Charge / accounting |
|---|---|---|
| ONYX QA defect | Extra/missing fingers, bad hands/anatomy/proportions, malformed eyes, severe face or identity artifact, damaged clothing, obvious AI defects, unusable output, missed explicit requirement | Free; never consumes a client round, including defects discovered after delivery |
| Client correction | Smile intensity, small stylistic adjustment, preferred compliant variation | Portrait 0, Signature 1, Premium 2 included rounds |
| Additional Correction Round | Consolidated same-scope feedback after included allowance | ADD_CORRECTION_ROUND, 700 RUB |
| Creative expansion | Major wardrobe/location/style change, new Concept, new Collection | Quote Additional Concept, Additional Collection or new order before work |

One round is one consolidated feedback list plus its agreed response. Log affected finals, request, classification, approval, round counter and result. No invented per-round image cap; no full-session reset or unlimited regeneration. Mixed feedback is split into free defects and subjective scope so defects cannot consume paid entitlement.

Keep source and earlier versions; repair/regeneration produces distinct versions. Re-run relevant technical/identity/anatomy checks and human final acceptance before replacement delivery. A paid Repair applies only to external images or scope outside ONYX obligations. If repair cannot pass, use replacement or the Service Standard resolution process; never promise an impossible repair.
