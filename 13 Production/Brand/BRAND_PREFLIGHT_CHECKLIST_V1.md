# ONYX Brand Preflight v1

- Is the photography the dominant element?
- Is the message limited to one clear idea?
- Is Manrope used for functional text and Cormorant Garamond used sparingly?
- Are colors limited to the ONYX palette?
- Is the approved editorial wordmark used instead of plain text over a photograph?
- Does a prepayment preview contain the order identifier and repeated watermark?
- Are paid delivery files completely clean?
- Are source and master files preserved without overwriting experimental assets?
- Are filenames, dimensions and delivery tier recorded in the package manifest?
