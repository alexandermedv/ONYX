# ONYX Monogram v1

Approved direction: high-contrast editorial `O` with a short centered diamond incision. The presentation concept uses black onyx and silk texture; production artwork is flat and transparent.

Use the monogram for avatars, favicons and compact marks. Use the lockup for covers and brand headers. Champagne `#B5A079` is the primary dark-background version; a monochrome version may use Onyx `#111111` or Warm White `#F6F4EF`.

Do not add glow, bevel, metallic gradients or a stone texture inside the production mark. The textured black-gold treatment belongs to presentation backgrounds only.

## Presentation artwork

`Presentations/ONYX_MONOGRAM_ONYX_SILK_PRESENTATION_V1.png` is an approved atmospheric treatment of the logo. It may be used for presentation covers, editorial collages, campaign moodboards and brand reveal materials. Keep the complete composition intact and do not extract its textured gold as production artwork.

Do not use the presentation artwork as a watermark, favicon, paid-delivery mark or small functional logo. Do not place body copy over the monogram or wordmark; reserve clear space around the central composition.
